GuestChat collects guest reviews from various **OTAs (Online Travel Agents)** — Booking.com, Agoda, Tiket.com, Traveloka, Expedia, and Trip.com — into a single dashboard, complete with automatic sentiment analysis.

## Reputation Dashboard

**Menu location:** the star icon in the left sidebar.

Filters available at the top: **Date Type** (all dates, or a custom date range) and **OTA Provider**. After clicking **Search**, the following three widgets update:

| Widget | Function |
|---|---|
| **Ratio** | Donut chart showing the proportion of Positive / Negative / Neutral reviews. |
| **Most Frequent Phrases** | A word cloud of the phrases guests mention most often, filterable by category (Location, Cleanliness, Service, etc.). |
| **Summary** | Summary cards of scores per category (Service, Location, Cleanliness, Value, Room, Design, Facilities, F&B, Other) with an Excellent/Stable/Bad/Needs Attention status. |

## All Reviews Page

**Menu location:** the **View OTA Reviews** button on the Reputation dashboard.

This page displays every review one by one, with more complete filters: **Date, OTA, Status (Analyzed/Unanalyzed), Category, Polarity**. The display can be switched between **Card** (grid) mode or **Tile** (list) mode.

## Review Detail & Reply

Click a review to open its details: original text + translation (if available), a list of **Detected Aspects** (category, specific opinion, and its polarity), plus previous reply history.

:::

:::danger[Only Reviews from Channex Can Be Replied To]
The reply form only appears for reviews received through **Channex**. For other sources, GuestChat shows the notice *"Replies are only available for reviews received through Channex"* with no reply form.
:::
